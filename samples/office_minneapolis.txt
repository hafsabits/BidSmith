SOLICITATION NUMBER: 47PF0026R0041
TITLE: Design-Build of New Federal Office Building, Minneapolis, MN
AGENCY: General Services Administration, Public Buildings Service, Region 5

SET-ASIDE: None. This acquisition is full and open competition.

DESCRIPTION:
The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) intends to award a single design-build
contract for a new four-story federal office building of approximately 180,000 gross square
feet at 300 South 4th Street, Minneapolis, Minnesota. The building will house multiple
federal tenant agencies and must achieve LEED Gold certification.

The estimated construction magnitude is between $60,000,000 and $85,000,000.

Offerors must demonstrate at least three comparable projects completed in the last
seven years and provide evidence of bonding capacity for the full contract value.

PROPOSALS DUE: Phase 1 submissions are due by 2:00 PM Central Time on January 15, 2027.
Questions must be submitted by December 18, 2026.
